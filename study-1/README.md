# Study 1: Scientometric Mapping

This directory contains the materials originally released for:

> Susnjak, T., Palffy, C., Zimina, T., Altynbekova, N., Garg, K., and Gilbert, L. (2026). Large language models for scientometric mapping of scientific controversy: A validated hybrid AI–Human framework. *Scientometrics*, 131, 4499–4532. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11192-026-05681-3

## Available materials

- `prescreening_prompt.txt`: prompt used for initial relevance screening.
- `main_prompt_a.txt`: primary stance-classification prompt.
- `main_prompt_b.txt`: self-reflection and classification-review prompt.
- `Interrater Training.docx`: training material used for expert classification and inter-rater evaluation.

These are the Study 1 materials made available through this repository. No additional Study 1 data, prompts, protocols, or code are planned for release here.

The source corpus of article abstracts is not redistributed because abstracts may be subject to publisher or author copyright.

See the [repository home page](../) for the preprint, published article, and abstract.
